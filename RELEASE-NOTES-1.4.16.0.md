# Fizz Health v1.4.16.0 — Podcasts Foundation

## Included
- Adds a Podcasts destination to the Settings landing page.
- Adds an isolated Podcasts module that does not depend on Health, Labs, Nutrition, or Recommendations data.
- Adds dedicated `podcasts`, `podcast_episodes`, `podcast_notes`, and `podcast_tags` tables.
- Adds the My Podcasts landing page, search, and empty state.
- Adds manual podcast creation and editing.
- Adds a podcast detail page with artwork, publisher, description, categories, RSS URL, website, and Apple Podcasts deep link.
- Adds soft removal from My Podcasts by setting the podcast inactive.

## Explicitly not included
- External podcast directory search.
- RSS parsing or episode synchronization.
- Audio streaming, downloading, playback, queues, or notifications.
- AI summaries, listening history, or background refresh.
