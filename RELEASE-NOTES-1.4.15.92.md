# Fizz Health v1.4.15.92 — Daily Brief Consolidation

## Objective

Make the Daily Brief the single structured source of meaningful health intelligence and simplify the Health page to current readings, history, and timeline information.

## Implemented

- Replaced the newest-first event feed with a 23-topic briefing document.
- Added a timestamped introduction and a complete topic index.
- Added newspaper-style section headlines, collapsed by default, with expandable details.
- Made the spoken briefing read the same section model, in the same order, including all details.
- Added current-weight historical context: previous reading, seven-day average, last date at or below the current weight, elapsed days, highest-weight change, and recorded-low recognition.
- Moved interpreted health drivers, longitudinal findings, trend confidence, biomarkers, risks, opportunities, Pantry status, shopping priorities, and monitoring status into the Daily Brief.
- Removed the Health Intelligence 2.0 summary, relationship cards, coaching correlations, biomarker strip, goal-intelligence list, and preventive-intelligence rows from the Health page.
- Reflowed the remaining Health content upward.
- Replaced the Health page masthead with the standard X / centered title / right-side placeholder header.

## Scope controls

No inventory, consumption, Prepared Recipe, shopping-functionality, nutrition-calculation, barcode, or unrelated page changes were introduced.
