# Fizz Health v1.4.13.4 — Menu Calendar and Meal Card Correction

Corrective release for FH-1413.3D and FH-1413.5C.

- Pins the Menu header and calendar to the actual viewport so they do not scroll away on iPhone Safari or the installed PWA.
- Removes the unapproved “Complete the course” controls and all Appetizer, Side, Dessert, Beverage, and Condiment buttons from planned-meal cards.
- Preserves each meal container as the single container for every food assigned to that meal.
